FIX playground

Decode & validate a FIX message.

Paste a raw FIX message below. It is decoded against the FIX 4.4 dictionary (field names, enum meanings) and checked for a valid checksum and body length, all in your browser, powered by fixwire. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Separators: SOH, |, ^, or newlines all work.
